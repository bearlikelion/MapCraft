# MapCraft Update

## Face Tool

- **New Feature:** Right click a face in the 3D view to give it the active face's material and alignment wrapped around the corner, as in Hammer; Shift+right click projects it and Ctrl+right click takes the material only.

## Materials

- Inspect Material on a plain picture opens New Material with its picture as the albedo, turning it into a PBR material of the same name.
- New Material updates a material whose name is already taken instead of refusing it, and says whether it replaces a PBR material or a plain picture.
- New Material accepts names with folders, such as the SDK's own material paths.

## Lighting

- Baked lighting keeps hex and macro tiling in the Lit view instead of showing a plain repeat; bake again to see it.
