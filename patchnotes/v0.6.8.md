# MapCraft Update

## Editing

- **New Feature:** **View > Paste at Pointer** drops a paste under the pointer instead of a grid step from the copy.
 In a 2D view the middle of what you copied goes to the point and its depth stays as it was; in the 3D view the copy stands on the face or ground under the pointer. It is on by default, and with Snap on the copy still shifts by whole grid steps.
- **Fix:** Duplicate (`Ctrl+D`) and Duplicate Linked (`Alt+D`) leave the copy exactly where the original is, instead of nudging it a grid step across the map.
