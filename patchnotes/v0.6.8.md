# MapCraft Update

## Editing

- **New Feature:** The Rotate tool steps by an amount you pick beside the tool buttons (Free, 1°, 5°, 15°, 45° or 90°, or one you type), instead of only snapping to 15° while Snap is on. Free turns with the pointer exactly as before.
- **New Feature:** The Scale tool gets a factor on each of x, y and z beside the tool buttons, each showing the size it would make; Enter or the tick applies them about the selection's center in one undo step.
- **New Feature:** **View > Paste at Pointer** drops a paste under the pointer instead of a grid step from the copy: in 2D at the pointer, in 3D standing on the face or ground under it, on by default.
- Duplicate and Duplicate Linked leave the copy exactly where the original is, instead of nudging it a grid step across the map.
