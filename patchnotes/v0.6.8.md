# MapCraft Update

Big thanks to Any-other, our first outside contributor, who built every change in this update!

## Editing

- **New Feature:** Rotate steps by an amount you pick beside the tool buttons: Free, 1°, 5°, 15°, 45°, 90° or one you type.
- **New Feature:** Scale takes an exact x, y and z factor beside the tool buttons, applied about the selection's center in one undo step.
- **New Feature:** View > Paste at Pointer drops a paste under the pointer, standing on the face or ground under it in the 3D view, on by default.
- Duplicate and Duplicate Linked leave the copy exactly where the original is instead of nudging it a grid step.
