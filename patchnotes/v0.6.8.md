# MapCraft Update

## Editing

- **Fix:** A compile packs the normal, roughness and metallic maps of your own materials instead of only their picture, so what is bumpy in MapCraft is bumpy in the game. A roughness or metallic kept in a combined `_orm` picture is written out as its own red-channel picture, the only channel the game reads those two slots from.
- **New Feature:** Maps beside a material's picture are found by name - `wall_normal.png`, `wall_roughness.png`, `wall_orm.png` - so a folder of loose pictures shades in the Lit view and in the game without a material file. Set **Lit maps** in the material's inspector to point them elsewhere, or off; that choice packs too.
