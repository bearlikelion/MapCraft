## Lighting

- **New Feature:** `_lightmap_bits` (worldspawn) keeps fewer mantissa bits of the baked light, 8 for the brightness and 7 for its directional bands by default. That is about a third off the size of a sunlit map's baked lighting for shading detail finer than the bake's own noise, and `9 9` keeps every bit again.
- **New Feature:** `_lightmap_budget` (worldspawn) is how many MB of baked lighting a map aims at, 64 by default: a map whose faces would take more than that is baked at a coarser lightmap scale instead of outrunning what a map's package can carry, and says what it settled on. `0` bakes as sharp as the map asks.
- Bake Lighting reports the MB it wrote in its status, and `mapcraft-cli bake` reports it against the budget, taking `--bits "8 7"` and `--budget 32`.
