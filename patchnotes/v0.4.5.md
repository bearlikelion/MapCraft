# MapCraft Update

## Selection

- **New Preference:** Edit > Preferences > Selection swaps click and double click in Groups mode, so a click selects the whole group and a double click one brush.
- **New Feature:** Double click an edge in the Vertex tool's edge mode to select its loop, or use Select Edge Loop.
- **New Feature:** Double click a face in the Face tool to pick the ring of faces around its brush, with the nearest edge setting the direction, or use Select Face Loop.

## Brushes

- Blocks drawn in a 2D view take their depth from the brushes last selected instead of the last block drawn.
- Extruding a face into a new brush shows the size of the new brush only.

## Materials

- **New Feature:** The Materials panel edits a custom material's tint, roughness, metallic, emission, normal strength, height and every texture slot, saving to its file as you go.
- Inspect Material picks each texture slot from the custom folder's pictures instead of only showing them.
- Picture lists in Lit maps, the Material inspector and New Material have a search field.
- Baked lighting keeps normal maps, roughness and metallic in the Lit view instead of turning surfaces flat a moment after a change.

## UV Editor

- **New Feature:** Arrow keys nudge the picked faces' textures by one grid step, or one texel with Shift, while the pointer is over the UV editor.

## Scene Tree

- The mouse wheel scrolls the scene tree while dragging a node, and holding the pointer near its top or bottom edge scrolls it too.
- Esc or letting go outside the scene tree cancels a drag.

## Surf

- The ramp Tip builds a slab under the ramp instead of flattening its surf angle; the default ramp is 480 tall with a 64 tip.
- Troughs with no tip no longer cover their floor with a skip face.
