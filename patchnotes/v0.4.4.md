# MapCraft Update

## Selection

- **New Feature:** Clicking a brush inside a group or brush entity selects just that brush; double click it to select the whole group or entity, as in TrenchBroom.
- Box select and drag picking in the 2D views pick single parts instead of whole groups.
- Clicking a mirrored copy selects the brush it copies; double click it to select the mirror group.

## Views

- **New Feature:** View > Two View Layout puts the 3D view beside one 2D view, and Space turns the 2D view to the next of Top, Front and Side.
- Shift+Space goes back to the two or four view layout it came from.
- Dragging in the 3D view with the Select tool anywhere off the selection draws a new block; dragging the selection still moves it.
- Alt+Tab no longer leaves the mouse stuck flying or dragging in MapCraft while another window has focus.

## Lighting

- Lighting baked before the views finish loading a map's materials no longer comes out at the wrong texture scale.
- Packed maps carry their baked lighting into Godot.

## SDKs

- SurfsUp SDK v9 bakes lighting.
