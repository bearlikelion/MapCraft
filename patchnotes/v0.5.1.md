# MapCraft Update

## SDKs

- **New SDK:** Generic Map opens Quake and TrenchBroom style maps from games that have no SDK of their own.
- Generic Map takes its textures from a folder of pictures or WADs, keeps every entity's class and keys, and exports back to a Valve220 map.

## Materials

- **New Feature:** KTX2 textures load as materials in SDKs that list them, in the BC1, BC3, BC5 and BC7 formats.
