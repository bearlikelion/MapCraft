# MapCraft Update

## Lighting

- **New Feature:** Baked Lighting: SDKs can bake lightmaps with MapCraft's own baker, including bounced light, the sun and glowing materials.
- **New Feature:** Run > Bake Lighting makes a full bake in the background, with a progress bar and Cancel in the status bar, and saves it beside the map.
- **New Feature:** Run > Automatic Light Bake keeps a quick draft of the lighting current in the Lit view as you edit.
- With automatic bakes off, a LIGHTING IS STALE warning with a Bake button shows once the map changes.
- The Lit view loads the saved bake when a map opens, as long as the map has not changed since.
- Compiling a map whose bake is missing or out of date bakes the lighting first, then packs it with the map.
- Godot shows packed maps with their baked lighting, and the sun lights only what moves, such as players.
- The command line tool can bake a map's lighting.

## Selection

- Small point entities stay easy to click when zoomed far out.
- Clicking in a 2D view picks the smallest object under the pointer instead of the wall in front.

## Inspector

- Entity keys that share a prefix, such as sun or fog, fold into their own collapsed section.

## Godot

- Fog density from the map is no longer reset to the default.
- Maps can hold four times as many picture pixels, sixteen 4096 by 4096 pictures.

## Other

- **New Feature:** View > Show FPS shows frame rate and draw times in the 3D views.
- Packing a map into its own folder no longer deletes the map.
