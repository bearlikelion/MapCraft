# MapCraft Update

## Models

- **New Feature:** Animation picker for model entities, played in Godot on Loop, Ping-pong or Once.
- **New Feature:** Selected model entities play their animation in the 3D view, skinned models included.
- **New Feature:** Dropping a .glb onto a view copies it beside the map and places it as a model where it lands.
- **New Feature:** Collision setting on prop_static: Convex by default, Trimesh for exact shapes, or None.
- The Scale tool rescales model entities, snapping to whole numbers with grid snapping on.
- Rotating a model on the X or Y ring tips it over instead of leaving it upright.
- .gltf models export into the map's asset pack as .glb.
- Animated and skinned models pack and load in Godot; shape keys are left out.
- Skinned models draw at their real size and pose, and models without texture coordinates no longer vanish in solid views.
- The Models tab lists your own models in a Custom folder.

## Materials

- **New Feature:** Material inspector: right click a material to live edit its tint, roughness, metallic, emission and normal settings, saved when you let go.
- **New Feature:** PBR materials gain emission energy, normal strength and a height map with height scale and a depth flip.
- **New Feature:** tools/skip and tools/clip materials, lettered SKIP and CLIP like TrenchBroom.
- See-through materials such as glass and grates show what lies behind them and draw only their front faces.
- Faces behind glass are no longer dropped from the game.
- Pictures with transparency draw see-through in Godot, cut out when every pixel is fully clear or solid.
- Emission maps light up in the Lit view as they do in Godot.
- The Materials browser lists your own materials in a Custom folder.
- Materials with spaces in their names export.

## Brushes and Entities

- **New Feature:** Right click brushes and pick Create Brush Entity to turn them into any brush class, grouped by prefix like TrenchBroom.
- **New Feature:** Clicking a brush class in the Entities tab draws every new block as that entity.
- Brush Type buttons fill in the class's default keys.
- Rotating shows where the selection started, the angle swept and the signed angle turned.
- Shortcut hints describe Ctrl+Alt+Shift drag, which extrudes a new brush along a face's normal.

## Surf

- **New Feature:** Surf Ramp Skip hidden faces, on by default, puts tools/skip on the faces between pieces so see-through ramps hide their insides.

## Lighting and Environment

- **New Feature:** Glow settings on light_environment, so emissive surfaces bloom in Godot.
- **New Feature:** Depth fog, height fog and aerial perspective on light_environment, previewed in the Lit view.
- Out of range environment values clamp to their limit instead of resetting, so a fog density of 100 shows full fog.

## SDKs

- SurfsUp SDK version 8 brings the new fog, glow, collision and tool material settings.
- Development builds use the SDKs in the source tree ahead of installed copies.
