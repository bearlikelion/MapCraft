# MapCraft Update

## EverQuest

- **New SDK:** EverQuest imports classic zones from their S3D archives, with the zone's textures, placed objects and lights, in the game's own coordinates.
- Zone geometry comes in as brushes, with drawn, walk-through and invisible-wall geometry each in its own group.
- Placed objects show their models from the zone's object archive with their heading, tilt and scale.
- Zone lights fade with EverQuest's own falloff curve, so baked zones light much as the game's do.
- The EverQuest SDK has its own logo in the SDK Manager.

## Lighting

- **New Feature:** The Falloff curve key on lights picks EverQuest's falloff, in the Lit view and in baked lighting.

## Other

- Very large imports no longer fail partway through.
