# MapCraft Update

## Editing

- Arrow keys in the 3D view move the selection one grid step along the nearest world axis.
- Alt with Up and Down in the 3D view raises and lowers the selection.
- **New Feature:** Tools > Extrude to Top Level places extruded brushes beside the original's entity instead of inside it.
- **New Feature:** Tools > Snap Vertices to Integers rounds every corner of the selected brushes to whole units.
