# MapCraft Update

## Displacements

- Displacements get baked lighting and cast baked shadows, in the editor's Lit view and in game; bake again to see it.
- Displacements stay visible in the Lit view instead of disappearing.
- Noise presets lay flat on displaced faces of every direction instead of folding faces at other angles into stripes.
- **New Feature:** Flat center keeps the noise to a band around the edges and flattens the middle, to box a level in.

## Materials

- Search finds materials in hidden folders too.
- Folders inside a hidden folder show as hidden.
- The Materials panel scrolls when its folders and material settings are open, so the materials stay in reach.
- Inspect Material on an SDK material brings its normal, roughness, metallic and emission maps into New Material.
- Inspect Material on a material the SDK's plugin draws, such as a Godot shader material, explains why it cannot become a PBR material.
- **New Feature:** Browse in the Material inspector's texture lists picks a picture anywhere and copies it beside the material.
- **New Feature:** Make PBR material in Lit maps opens New Material with the maps picked there.
- Lit maps says it only changes the editor's Lit view.

## 2D Views

- Zooming no longer changes the grid snapping; a zoomed out view draws fewer lines but still snaps to the grid size.
- Minus and plus change the grid size in every keymap.
