MapCraft

The level editor that combines Hammer and TrenchBroom with a Godot-style scene tree. Draw brushes in four views, place surf ramps that stay editable, and play your map in your game with one click.

Download latest

  • Windows64-bit zip
  • LinuxAppImage
  • Godot addonFor Godot 4.4+

Free and open source, with the SurfsUp, Counter-Strike: Source and Quake SDKs built in. No macOS build yet: build it from source.

What it does

  • Four views, one scene tree

    Top, front, side and 3D, laid out like Hammer.

  • Surf ramps that stay editable

    Change a ramp's shape any time, and it rebuilds.

  • The brush tools you know

    Clip, carve, hollow and more, previewed first.

  • See the lighting as you build

    Lit mode shades PBR materials under your map's sky and sun.

  • Sculpt terrain

    Paint mountains, or load a heightmap.

  • Shape brushes like in Blender

    Bevel edges, edit UVs, sweep along Bezier paths.

  • Build half, mirror the rest

    Mirrors, linked copies and prefabs stay in sync.

  • Bring your muscle memory

    Hammer and TrenchBroom key presets, or your own.

  • One editor, every game

    Compile for CS:S with Valve's tools, or play in SurfsUp.

Your first map

Full walkthrough with pictures

  1. Download

    Unzip on Windows. On Linux, make the AppImage executable and run it.

  2. Pick your game

    File > New Map, then pick SurfsUp, Counter-Strike: Source or Quake.

  3. Point it at your game

    Choose your game's folder when SDK Settings asks.

  4. Build and play

    Press B to draw a block, add a spawn, then press Run.
